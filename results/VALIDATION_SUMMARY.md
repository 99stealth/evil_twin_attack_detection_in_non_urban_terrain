| w | False alarms/h | Detection rate (48 sessions) | Median delay (s) | Row-level recall |
|---|---|---|---|---|

0.974 to 0.806 (rows near a session's start don't yet have a full window and can't fire).

## V6 — Session-level permutation test

1000 permutations, primary protocol, fixed `ExtraTreesClassifier(n_estimators=300)`
(a fixed, untuned model independent of any hold-out design — F1 does not apply here).
Full table: `results/permutation_tests.csv`; figure: `figures/permutation_antenna.png`.

| Task | Observed acc | Null mean | Null 95th pct | p-value |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Point Prediction | 0.736 | 0.102 | 0.180 | 0.001 |
| Antenna Prediction | 0.336 | 0.170 | 0.243 | 0.002 |

Both **significantly above chance** (p < 0.005). Antenna, permuted only within sessions
sharing the same point (acceptance check #3 — verified in the notebook's last cell):
even though its leave-one-point-out accuracy (0.336) looks low against naive 1/6 = 0.167
chance, the session-permutation null (which preserves the point-conditional class
balance) is centered at 0.170 — so 0.336 is roughly double the *true* null expectation.

## V7 — No-imputation sensitivity variants (F2: both options reported)

Two variants against the main imputed dataset (2131 rows, 571 attack, 48 sessions):
**`dropna_5s`** (5s grid, `dropna()` instead of nearest-neighbour interpolation) and
**`scan_cycle`** (option a: for each anchor-sensor scan, nearest scan of the other
sensors within ±3.6s, row dropped if any sensor has no scan in that window — no
resampling grid, no imputation). Full table: `results/no_imputation_variant.csv`.

| Variant | Rows (imputed → variant) | Attack sessions kept |
|---|---|---|
| `dropna_5s` | 2131 → 442 (attack-only: 571 → 116) | 37 / 48 |
| `scan_cycle` | 2131 → 1277 (attack-only: 571 → 320) | 48 / 48 |

| Task | Model | Acc (imputed) | Acc (`dropna_5s`) | Δ | Acc (`scan_cycle`) | Δ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Attack Detection | KNN | 0.988 | 0.975 | −0.013 | 0.998 | +0.010 |
| Point Prediction | KNN | 0.743 | 0.750 | +0.007 | 0.763 | +0.020 |
| Antenna Prediction | XGBoost | 0.363 | 0.388 | +0.025 | 0.313 | −0.050 |

`dropna_5s` discards 11 of 48 attack sessions entirely because it forces alignment onto
an artificial 5s grid; `scan_cycle` keeps all 48 sessions and nearly 3x more rows, since
it aligns on the sensors' own ~7.3s scan cadence instead. Imputation is not inflating the
headline numbers in either direction beyond ordinary CV noise: deltas are within
±0.03 except Antenna/`scan_cycle` (−0.05, still well inside Antenna's wide main-table CI
of [0.260, 0.473]).

From the Point Prediction OOF predictions of the main-table winning model (KNN, FE,
primary protocol, leave-one-antenna-out), using `eval_protocol.point_distance_m` (chord
on the 11m circle, 45° spacing, points numbered counter-clockwise from the east). Full

| Level | n | Mean error (m) | Median error (m) | Exact | Adjacent (8.42m) | Farther | 95% CI mean error (m) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sample | 571 | 3.32 | 0.0 | 74.3% | 14.2% | 11.6% | [2.38, 4.29] |
| Session majority vote (n=48) | 48 | 1.03 | 0.0 | 89.6% | 8.3% | 2.1% | — |

Session-level aggregation cuts the mean localization error by ~3x (3.32m → 1.03m),
consistent with the accuracy gain reported in V5. The 95% CI (session cluster
bootstrap, 2000 iterations, seed 0) on the sample-level mean error is [2.38, 4.29] m —
well under one point-spacing (8.42m) even at its upper bound.
